Features

Small, yet mighty.

Unlike classic browsers, ad blocking, your encrypted vault and privacy are not extensions — they are core.

Quantum Password Vault

Locked by a master password; Argon2id + AES-256-GCM. Plaintext passwords never touch disk.

Forced HTTPS

Plain HTTP is blocked with a "this site is not secure" interstitial and a one-time "visit anyway" option.

Ad & Tracker Blocker

~110 known networks cut at Chromium level; the counter shines on the shield chip in the tab strip.

Vertical Tab Bar

Arc-style sidebar; drag-drop groups, pinning, duplication, Ctrl+Shift+T to reopen closed tabs.

FTS5 Full-Text Search

History and bookmarks searched in milliseconds; bulk select & delete in one click.

Session Restore

Close it — your tabs come back. "Pick up where I left off" is always on.

Multi-Profile & Incognito

Profiles isolated in separate WebView2 data directories; incognito tabs write nothing to disk.

Light / Dark / System Theme

Live sync — switch theme and every internal page follows instantly; accent color is yours.

quantum:// Internal Pages

Settings, History, Bookmarks, Downloads, Profiles, Vault — all in tabs, like Chrome's internal pages.

Local Download Manager

Into Downloads/QuantumBrowser; finished and in-flight items under Settings → Downloads.

Zero Telemetry

Your behavior never leaves this device; there isn't even a server to send it to.

Open Source · AGPL-3.0

Written in Rust + React + Tauri; audit it, fork it, send a PR.

F.A.Q.

Questions?

SmartScreen says "PC protected your device". What now?

Choose More info → Run anyway. QuantumBrowser is unsigned, and this is Microsoft's standard control for unknown unsigned apps. Signing is the v1.0 target; until then the source code and the SHA256 are your assurance.

Can I use my Chrome extensions?

No — WebView2 does not expose the Chromium extension model. Most extension jobs (ad blocking, passwords, downloads) are built in.

Where is my data stored?

In SQLite files under %LOCALAPPDATA%\com.quantum.browser: history, bookmarks, settings, profiles and vault.db (your vault — encrypted with your master password). Nothing is ever sent anywhere.

What if I forget my master password?

The vault is unrecoverable by design — key derivation is one-way. Only you can open it; note your master password somewhere safe.

No WebView2 on my machine?

Windows 11 has it built in. On Windows 10 the installer downloads and installs it automatically.

How do I update?

Download the new version from the release page and install over. Auto-update is planned for v1.0.
